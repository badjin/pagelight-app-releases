# Pagelight

**A fast, quiet PDF viewer for your Mac, with an edit mode for your own text and handwriting.**

Pagelight opens PDFs in tabs, lets you jump around with thumbnails, outlines, bookmarks and search, and, when you want to mark a page up, adds your own text boxes and pen strokes on top. The original file is never changed: your additions are written only into a copy, through Save As.

## What you get

- **Tabs and windows.** Open several PDFs in tabs, or in separate windows. Open Recent and Open Quickly (Shift-Command-O) bring back files you used before.
- **A home screen of recent files.** Each file is a card with a thumbnail and the page you were on (for example `7/20`). Switch between list and grid, and filter by name.
- **Find your way around.** Page thumbnails, the document outline, your own bookmarks, and a find panel. Star a file to keep it in a Starred tab.
- **Reading controls.** Zoom, rotate, single-page and two-page layouts, a cover-page option, and a slide show.
- **Password-protected files.** Pagelight asks for the password and opens the file.
- **Document properties and printing.** Printing includes the text and pen strokes you added.
- **Looks.** Five themes (Light, Dark, Starry Blue, Mint Green and Auto) and eight page background colours, or none.

## Edit mode

Press **Edit** in the left rail (or choose **View > Edit Mode**) to switch from reading to marking up a page.

- **Text.** Click a page and type. Choose colour and size, then move, resize or delete the box. Korean text works, typed or pasted.
- **Pen.** Draw freehand strokes; choose the colour and thickness.
- **Undo and Redo** for every change.
- **Save As only.** Your additions are written into a new copy. The file you opened is never modified, and if you choose its own name, Pagelight refuses and tells you why. Closing a tab with unsaved changes asks whether to save a copy.
- **Existing content stays untouched.** Notes, form fields and links already in the PDF are not moved or changed, and you cannot fill in forms.

## Requirements

- A Mac with macOS 13 or later.

## Install

1. Download `Pagelight-<version>.dmg` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and open it. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
2. Drag `Pagelight.app` to your `Applications` folder and open it.
3. Open a PDF with **File > Open**.

## Good to know

- Pagelight is a viewer plus one edit mode. It has no AI, text recognition, signing, file conversion or cloud features, and it does not edit the text or images already in a PDF.
- Edit mode is switched off for password-locked files and for files whose owner password forbids editing. A page that has its own rotation cannot take added text or drawings; Pagelight shows a note at the foot of that page.
- A Korean text box on a page that is rotated in the view wraps onto two lines.
- Once, a window was seen to disappear during testing. It was not reproduced, and the cause is not known.
